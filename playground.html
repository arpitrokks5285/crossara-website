Disclaimer↓

CROSSARA Labs Playground is just a small interactive extra we made for the website — something you can play around with while checking out CROSSARA and some of its crosshair designs.

The Playground is separate from the CROSSARA Android app. The targets, score, movement, shooting controls and other game-like behaviour you see here are only part of this website experience. They are not meant to show or promise gameplay, controls, scoring, target behaviour or any other feature inside the actual app.

Some CROSSARA crosshair artwork is used here simply to make the Playground feel familiar and to let visitors see different designs in a more playful way. The real app is a crosshair overlay utility, and its actual features, controls and behaviour should be understood from the app itself and the product information on this website.

Your Playground score is temporary. We do not treat it as an account score or leaderboard result, and refreshing or reopening the page starts the Playground fresh again.
