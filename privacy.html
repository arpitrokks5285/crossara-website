Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026
Contact: crossarasupport@gmail.com

Last Updated: August 24, 2026

CROSSARA LABS built Crossara as an Android visual crosshair overlay application. This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you use Crossara, what stays on your device, when Google services may process information, and the choices available to you.

Our approach is to keep Crossara's core functionality local to your device wherever practical. CROSSARA LABS does not currently operate a Crossara account system, proprietary user database, analytics backend, or cloud storage service for user content.

If you have questions about this policy, you can contact us at crossarasupport@gmail.com.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

Crossara stores information locally when necessary to remember your settings and provide app functionality.

This can include your selected crosshair, size, opacity, color, rotation and position; recently used crosshairs; tutorial state; purchase-entitlement state; rewarded-access expiration times; advertising-related app state; and a reference to a custom image you select.

This locally stored information is used to operate Crossara. CROSSARA LABS does not upload these settings to its own server.

Crossara's current configuration also excludes its SharedPreferences from Crossara-configured cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

2. Custom PNG Images

Eligible users can select their own PNG image for use as a custom crosshair.

Image selection takes place through Android's system file picker. Crossara stores a reference to the selected file and may retain Android's permission to access that file so that the image remains available for use.

The selected image is decoded and rendered locally on your device. Crossara does not copy or upload the selected image to a CROSSARA LABS server.

Selecting one image does not give Crossara unrestricted access to unrelated files on your device.

3. Overlay and Foreground Service

Crossara requires Android's Display over other apps access to provide its core crosshair-overlay functionality.

The overlay is a non-interactive visual layer. Crossara does not use the overlay to read another application's screen contents, capture screenshots, record your screen, read another application's memory, inject input, or automate gameplay.

When you activate the crosshair, Crossara uses an Android foreground service to keep the user-requested overlay active. Android may display a persistent notification while this service is running, and the overlay can be stopped by the user.

4. Advertising

The free version of Crossara uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob).

Crossara currently uses advertising formats including banner advertisements and rewarded advertisements. Rewarded advertisements can be voluntarily completed to receive temporary access to eligible Crossara content.

Google's advertising technology may process information such as your IP address, which can be used to estimate general location; interactions with the app and advertisements; diagnostic and performance information; and device or advertising identifiers. Google documents these categories as part of the Google Mobile Ads SDK's operation.

This processing occurs through Google's advertising services. CROSSARA LABS does not operate its own advertising-profile database and does not receive every category of information that Google may process through its services.

Under Crossara's current entitlement model, PLUS and ULTIMATE users bypass Crossara's normal advertising.

5. Advertising Consent and Privacy Choices

Crossara uses the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage applicable advertising consent and privacy choices.

Depending on your location, Google's requirements and applicable privacy rules, you may be presented with options concerning advertising and related data processing.

Where available, applicable choices can be reviewed again through:

Crossara → Settings → Privacy Choices

The options shown may differ between countries or regions. Crossara does not promise that the same consent interface or choices will be available to every user worldwide.

6. Google Play Purchases

Crossara offers optional one-time purchases through Google Play Billing, including Crossara PLUS and Crossara ULTIMATE.

Google Play processes the financial transaction. CROSSARA LABS does not receive or store your complete credit-card number, debit-card number, bank-account credentials, CVV, PIN, or similar payment authentication information.

Crossara receives purchase-related information needed to determine and restore the entitlement associated with a valid purchase. An entitlement state may also be stored locally on your device so the app can provide the features associated with your purchase.

Google's handling of payment and transaction information is governed by Google's applicable policies and terms.

7. Google and Other Third-Party Services

Crossara currently integrates Google services used for specific app functions, including:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for advertising
  • Google User Messaging Platform for advertising consent and privacy choices
  • Google Play Billing for purchases; and
  • Google Play functionality used for app rating/review experiences.

These services may process information under Google's own privacy policies and service terms.

The fact that a Google service processes information does not necessarily mean CROSSARA LABS receives or stores that information itself.

Google Play requires developers to account for data handled through third-party SDKs when maintaining their privacy and Data Safety disclosures.

8. Analytics and Crash Reporting

Crossara does not currently integrate Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics, or a proprietary CROSSARA LABS analytics or crash-reporting platform.

Google services integrated for advertising, billing, consent or Play functionality may nevertheless process diagnostic, interaction or service-related information as part of providing those services.

If Crossara later introduces separate analytics, crash reporting, or similar data-processing technology, we will review and update this Privacy Policy and applicable Google Play disclosures as necessary.

9. Accounts and Personal Information

You do not currently need a CROSSARA LABS account to use Crossara.

Crossara does not currently ask you to create a username or password or provide your name, phone number or email address as part of a Crossara account.

Use of Google Play and information associated with your Google account are governed by Google's systems and policies. Crossara does not create a separate Crossara profile from your Google Play account.

10. Feedback and Support

If you choose Send Feedback, Crossara may open an email application with useful context such as the Crossara app version.

You decide whether to send the message and what information to include.

If you contact CROSSARA LABS, we will receive the information you voluntarily send, which can include your email address, the contents of your message and any attachments you choose to provide.

Support correspondence may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to you, maintain appropriate business records, investigate abuse or technical issues, resolve disputes, or meet applicable legal obligations.

Please do not send passwords, complete payment-card information or other sensitive information that is unnecessary for us to assist you.

11. Children and Teen Users

Crossara is intended for users aged 13 and older. Crossara's current Google Play target audience does not include children under 13.

Crossara does not operate a proprietary account system through which CROSSARA LABS knowingly requests names, email addresses, phone numbers or similar account information from children.

Users under the age of legal majority in their country should use Crossara with the involvement or permission of a parent or legal guardian where required by applicable law.

Advertising, consent and Google Play services may be subject to additional age- and region-related requirements administered through Google and applicable platform settings.

12. Data Retention and Removal

Crossara's settings and related local state generally remain on your device until they are reset, replaced, expire where applicable, the application's data is cleared, or Crossara is uninstalled.

Reset Crosshair Settings resets applicable Crossara preferences. It is not intended to delete purchase records maintained by Google Play.

Uninstalling Crossara normally removes Crossara-owned local application data according to Android's application-data behavior.

Crossara currently has no CROSSARA LABS cloud account or proprietary online user database, so there is no separate Crossara cloud account to request deletion of.

Information independently maintained by Google, your email provider or another third-party service is subject to that provider's own retention and deletion practices.

13. Security

Crossara is designed to minimize the amount of information handled directly by CROSSARA LABS and to keep core app settings on the user's device.

Google documents data collected by its Mobile Ads SDK as encrypted in transit using TLS. Other Google services use their own security practices and infrastructure.

No electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, and CROSSARA LABS does not make an absolute guarantee of security.

14. International Users and Privacy Rights

Crossara is intended for distribution in multiple countries.

Privacy, advertising and consumer requirements differ between jurisdictions. Depending on your location, Google UMP and other platform mechanisms may provide region-specific privacy choices.

Where applicable law gives you rights relating to information directly controlled by CROSSARA LABS, you may contact us at crossarasupport@gmail.com.

For information controlled independently by Google or another service provider, the relevant provider's privacy controls or request mechanisms may apply.

Nothing in this Privacy Policy is intended to limit rights that cannot legally be limited under applicable law.

15. Changes to Crossara's Data Practices

Crossara may evolve over time.

If we introduce functionality that materially changes how information is handled—for example, a Crossara account system, proprietary backend or cloud storage, additional analytics, a new advertising provider, or new permissions involving personal information—we will review this Privacy Policy and update applicable disclosures and consent mechanisms as required.

We do not describe hypothetical future data collection as though it occurs today.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Crossara's features, data practices, third-party services, legal obligations or platform requirements change.

The Last Updated date at the top of this page will identify the latest revision.

Where appropriate, material changes will also be reflected through relevant in-app disclosures, consent mechanisms or Google Play declarations.
